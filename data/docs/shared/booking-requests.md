# Activity Booking Requests

## How a request works
The concierge never confirms a booking. It sends a request to the activities desk with status PENDING. A staff
member approves or declines it, usually within 2 hours between 08:00 and 20:00. You'll see the update in your
chat, and the front desk can confirm it by phone.

## Payment
Paid activities are charged to your room once staff approve the request. Nothing is charged for a request that is
pending or declined. Free activities are still booked through a request so we can plan staffing.

## Cancellations
You can cancel an approved activity free of charge up to 4 hours before it starts, through the front desk.
Later cancellations and no-shows are charged in full, except for the kids club, which is always free.

## Weather
Outdoor activities can be cancelled for weather. If we cancel, you're never charged, and the activities desk will
offer another session.
