# Kids Club Policy - Mountain Lodge

## Ages and supervision
Nature explorers is for children aged 5 to 12. Children under 5 can't be admitted. One team member for every
5 children; all are first-aid and wilderness-first-aid trained.

## Drop-off and pick-up
Sign in and out at the kids cabin. Sessions run 09:00 to 12:00.

## What to bring
Closed shoes, a warm layer and a water bottle. We lend rain jackets.

## Price
Free for resident guests. Please request a place through the concierge; groups are small.
