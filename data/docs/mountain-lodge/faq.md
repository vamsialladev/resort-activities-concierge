# Guest FAQ - Mountain Lodge

## Guided trek
The morning trek is 7 km with 350 m of climbing and takes about 3 hours. It's for ages 10 and up. Hiking boots are
required; we lend boots in most sizes at the trailhead lobby. Bring water and a warm layer.

## Altitude
The lodge is at 2,400 m. Take it easy on your first day and drink plenty of water.

## Stargazing
The stargazing walk is for ages 8 and up and runs on clear nights only. We cancel by 18:00 if the sky is cloudy.

## Hot tub
The outdoor hot tub is open 16:00 to 22:00, ages 16 and up.

## Dogs
Dogs are welcome in cabin rooms only, up to 2 per room.
