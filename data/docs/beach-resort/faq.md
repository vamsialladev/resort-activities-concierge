# Guest FAQ - Beach Resort

## Pools
The main pool is open 07:00 to 21:00. The adults-only pool (18+) is open 09:00 to 19:00. Lifeguards are on duty
09:00 to 18:00.

## Sunset cruise
The cruise leaves the main pier at 18:00 and returns at 19:30. All ages are welcome; children under 12 must be
with an adult. Life jackets are provided for everyone, in child sizes too. The cruise doesn't run on Mondays.

## Snorkelling
Snorkel tours are for ages 8 and up. Guests under 16 must be with an adult. Gear and reef-safe sunscreen are
included.

## Towels
Pool and beach towels are free at the towel hut by the main pool.

## Dining with children
Children under 12 eat for half price at high tea and at the buffet. High chairs are available everywhere.
