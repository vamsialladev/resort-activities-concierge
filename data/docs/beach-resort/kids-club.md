# Kids Club Policy - Beach Resort

## Ages and supervision
The kids club is for children aged 4 to 11. Children under 4 can't be admitted, even with a sibling. Our staff
ratio is one team member for every 6 children, and every team member is first-aid trained.

## Drop-off and pick-up
A parent or guardian signs each child in and out at the kids club pavilion. Children can stay up to 3 hours per
session. Please be back at the pavilion at the session's end time.

## Health and allergies
Tell the team about allergies at sign-in. Snacks are nut-free. Children with a fever or a stomach bug in the last
24 hours can't attend.

## Price
The kids club is free for resident guests. Places are limited, so please request a place through the concierge.

## What to bring
Sun hat, swimwear, a towel and reef-safe sunscreen. We reapply sunscreen every 90 minutes.
